# Batch 002 dividend-source review rubric

Version: batch-002-dividend-review-rubric-v1. Applies only to frozen controls 26–50 (batch-002), assessed before batch scoring. Existing development-confidence-review-policy-v2 and its cutoff of 90 remain unchanged.

These are component-level documentary judgments. A certification, contract payment, option-valuation dividend assumption, taxonomy label, script or stylesheet is not automatically a shareholder dividend declaration. Filing form alone does not exclude a dividend action in a periodic report or amendment; read the supplied component.

A full-source component-scoped negative with resolved identity/scope receives classification 97 and evidence 98. The longer termination agreement and quarterly report receive 95/95 because their contractual/valuation context needs more interpretation. No dividend baseline is required to establish that a completely reviewed certification or other component makes no declaration.

Incomplete annual/quarterly review remains UNRESOLVED with PARTIAL_SOURCE_REVIEWED, even if a keyword search returns no match or a reviewed passage says no dividend is planned. Targeted explicit-policy reading receives 75/85; header/opening review plus empty keyword search receives 50/75. Neither is a completed full-source negative.

Unsupported XBRL presentation, label and definition linkbases and the XBRL instance remain UNRESOLVED at 50/75 pending complete technical coverage and historical schema authority. Root/namespace/role evidence establishes their provisional documentary role only. No external references or source scripts are fetched or executed. Structural inspection does not certify XSD semantics.

Overall score is min(classification,evidence). Unresolved classifications, partial coverage and unsupported/schema issues always remain in the review queue regardless of score. Scores are ordinal operational support, not probabilities. No threshold fitting or human calibration follows from this batch.

Route six incomplete cases to technical research follow-up: full source coverage for records 29 and 37; unsupported XBRL/schema authority for records 43–46. They remain flagged in the registered routing ledger. This worklist does not manufacture a human classification decision or close a gate.

Do not infer initiation from missing earlier history, an increase from unverified arithmetic, an ordinary dividend from option assumptions, a new disclosure from an amendment's filing date, or public dissemination from an SEC/printed date. Retain every frozen record and preserve all five families and all existing gates.
